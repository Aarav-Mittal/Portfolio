Full-Stack AI • Web Application Completed Project

Parity — AI Money Tracker & Run-Out Forecast

College students and young adults living on fixed stipends often face end-of-month financial panic. Traditional personal finance apps prioritize net-worth tracking and stock feeds; Parity solves the daily survival question: "Will my money last until my next allowance, and what can I safely spend today?"

Deterministic Forecasting Engine: 100% pure TypeScript arithmetic based on minor currency units (paise: Math.round(rupees * 100)) to completely eliminate floating-point rounding bugs—zero LLM hallucination of financial math. Verified by 96 Vitest unit tests.
Conversational AI Expense Logger: Parses unstructured inputs in everyday student vernacular (e.g. "spent 120 on auto", "chai 30") backed by Groq Cloud (qwen/qwen3.8-27b) and Google Gemini with an offline regex fallback.
What-If Purchase Simulator & Obligations: Automatically isolates committed upcoming bills (hostel rent, Wi-Fi, recharge) and tests irregular purchase affordability before money is spent.
